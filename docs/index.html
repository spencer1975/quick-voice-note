How it works

Three steps, and the third one is optional

01
Tap the micOn your phone it's the red mic in the note header. On desktop it's the ribbon. Or bind a phone shortcut so your Action Button starts recording before Obsidian is even on screen.
02
TalkLong-form recording, not dictation. Walk, pause, think, keep going. A safety limit stops and saves the recording if you forget it's running.
03
Read it tonightStop saves the audio and adds a timestamped entry to your daily note or the note you're in, with the transcript as plain text you can search, link and edit.

Looks the way you want it to

  • Pick a memo style: list item, voice callout card, quote, heading, paragraph or one line.
  • Keep the audio player in the note, link to the file, or transcript only.
  • Choose the card colour and wash to suit your theme.
  • Or edit the template and write your own.

Made for the field

For people whose best thinking happens outside

Farmers walking paddocks. Horticulturalists between rows. Anyone whose ideas arrive mid-stride and are gone by the time they're back at a desk.

  • It doesn't cut you off. System dictation stops when you pause. This records until you tap stop.
  • The audio is the backup. If the transcript misses a cultivar name, the recording is right there.
  • It files itself. Every memo lands in the note you'll be looking at tonight, timestamped.
  • Your vault, your files. Plain markdown and audio files. Nothing locked in a service.

Phone shortcut

Record from your pocket

Point a one-tap shortcut at obsidian://voice-note and Obsidian opens with recording already running. One tap stops and saves.

  • iPhone: install the Field Record shortcut below, then assign it to the Action Button or add it to Control Centre.
  • Android: any shortcut or automation app that can open a URL.
  • NFC tag: stick one on the ute dashboard or the shed door.
Download the Field Record shortcut 
# the URL behind the shortcut
obsidian://voice-note?action=record

# optional: don't auto-start
obsidian://voice-note?autostart=0

Privacy

Your audio stays in your vault

Recordings are files in your vault, synced however your vault syncs. They leave your device only if transcription is on, and then only to the endpoint you chose.

  • Your own API key is stored in the plugin's local data file and sent only to your transcription endpoint.
  • The cloud key sends audio to our transcription proxy, which forwards it for transcription and returns the text. Audio is not stored.
  • No analytics, no accounts, no tracking on this page.
